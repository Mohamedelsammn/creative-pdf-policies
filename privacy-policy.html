Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Creative PDF handles your documents and information. In short: Creative PDF works offline. Your documents are processed and stored on your device, and the app does not upload, transmit or sync them to any server.

What the app handles

Creative PDF works with the content you give it: documents and images you import, pages you scan, documents you create or edit, text recognised from your pages, signatures you draw, and your app settings (language and appearance).

Passwords you enter to protect or unlock a PDF are used only for that operation, held in memory, and never saved.

Creative PDF has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information.

On-device processing

Every tool, including scanning, conversion, compression, protection, PDF editing and text recognition (OCR), runs entirely on your device. Text recognition uses language data built into the app for English and Arabic. No document, page image, recognised text, file name or password is sent anywhere.

Camera

The camera permission is requested the first time you open the scanner. The camera is used only while the scanner is open on screen, never in the background, and no audio is recorded. Captured pages are saved in the app's private storage as part of your scan. You can withdraw camera access at any time in Android Settings; the other tools keep working.

Importing, saving and sharing files

Creative PDF does not request access to all files on your phone. It reads only the files and photos you choose in Android's file or photo picker.

“Save to device” writes a copy to the location you choose. “Share” and “Open” hand a copy to the app you choose. Once a copy leaves Creative PDF, it is handled by that location or app under its own terms and privacy policy.

Storage and retention

Your documents, saved signatures, unsaved Word drafts and settings are kept in Creative PDF's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

They stay there until you delete them. Deleted documents move to Trash, where they remain until you restore them or delete them permanently. Temporary working files are removed when a task ends or the next time the app starts. Uninstalling Creative PDF or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes all of its data; copies you saved elsewhere or shared are not affected.

Backups

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Creative PDF, so the app does not copy your documents to a backup account or to a new phone. To keep a copy, use Save to device or Share.

Network, analytics and advertising

Creative PDF does not request internet access and does not connect to any server. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. If an error occurs, the app may write a short technical line, without file names or document content, to your phone's system log; the app does not send it anywhere.

Third-party components

Creative PDF includes open-source components that run on your device, among them PDFium and PDFBox-Android for PDF processing, Tesseract for text recognition, OpenCV for page detection, and Flutter. They are used offline inside the app and do not send data to their authors. Their licences are listed under Settings › About Creative PDF › Open-source licenses.

Your choices

You decide what goes into the app and what leaves it. You can rename, export, share or delete any document, empty the Trash, delete saved signatures, withdraw camera access, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app at any time. Because the app collects no personal information, there is no account or server copy to request or delete.

Children

Creative PDF does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
