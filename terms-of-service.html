The app

Creative PDF is a document tool for Android. It scans documents, converts images, PDF and Word files, organises, edits, signs, compresses and protects PDFs, and recognises text in pages. It works offline, and all processing happens on your device.

Permitted use

You may use Creative PDF for personal or business purposes in line with applicable law. Use it only with documents you own or are allowed to work with. Do not use it to infringe the rights of others or to open documents you are not authorised to access. Unlock PDF removes a password only when you enter it; the app never guesses or bypasses passwords.

Your documents

Your documents remain yours. You are responsible for their content and for keeping your own copies of important files. Creative PDF cannot recover a forgotten password for a protected PDF, and files deleted permanently from Trash cannot be restored.

Results and limitations

Results depend on the source material. In particular: PDF to Word focuses on text, so layout, fonts and columns are not kept; text recognition can contain errors, especially on low-quality photos; automatic page detection may need manual correction; Word documents with unsupported content are simplified after a warning; and some older PDF readers cannot open files protected with AES-256. Check the output before you rely on it, especially for legal, financial or official documents.
